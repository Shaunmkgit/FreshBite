About FreshBite

Making delicious food easier to discover, order, and enjoy — all from one simple food delivery experience.

Our Story

FreshBite is a frontend food delivery platform created to bring restaurants, delicious meals, and customers together in one convenient place.

Our goal is to make browsing food simple and enjoyable. From discovering restaurants to selecting your favourite dishes and completing checkout, FreshBite provides a smooth ordering experience.

This project demonstrates a modern food delivery website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with browser localStorage for simulated application data.
