Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Choose a restaurant, select your food, add it to your cart, and complete the checkout form.

Can I track my order?

Yes. After checkout, use the Track Order option to see the simulated order progress.

How do coupons work?

Enter an available coupon code during checkout. Eligible discounts will automatically be applied to your order.
